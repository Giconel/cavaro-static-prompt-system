# Brand Visual Rules — Cavaro

## Brand Identity In One Line

Cavaro should feel calm, feminine, trustworthy, and polished — like a premium brand that real women actually wear.

---

## What Cavaro Looks Like

**Tone**
- Quiet confidence, not loud
- Warm and approachable, not cold and editorial
- Premium-lite — high quality feel without luxury price signalling
- Real and lived-in, not aspirational fantasy

**Colour Direction**
- Soft, warm, and natural palettes in backgrounds and settings
- Neutral tones: warm whites, warm greys, sage, stone, terracotta, dusty blue, soft cream
- Avoid cold blues, neon, over-saturated, or clinical colour grading

**Light**
- Natural daylight preferred
- Slightly warm or golden light where possible
- Imperfect but flattering — not studio lighting, not harsh direct sun
- Avoid dark moody lighting, dramatic shadows, or cinematic colour grades

**Texture and Surfaces**
- Real surfaces — textured concrete, worn brick, timber, natural stone, grass, café tiles
- Avoid perfectly clean, perfectly smooth, or overly designed backgrounds
- The setting should feel like it exists, not like it was art directed

**Photography Feel**
- UGC-style, like a genuine phone photo taken by someone who knows the subject
- Slightly imperfect framing is good
- Natural depth of field — soft background, clear subject
- No lens flares, no creative filters, no editorial styling choices

---

## What Cavaro Does Not Look Like

- Fast fashion — Shein, Boohoo, AliExpress
- Gen Z styling — trend-led, ironic, Y2K, micro-trends
- Luxury editorial — Vogue, Net-a-Porter, Saint Laurent vibes
- Influencer aesthetic — perfectly curated, over-posed, hyper-produced
- Cheap dropshipping — white backgrounds, bad lighting, supplier photos
- Studio photography — seamless backdrops, studio flash, posed model shots

---

## Visual Trust Signals

These details make Cavaro ads feel trustworthy to the target customer:

- Fabric texture is visible and looks real
- The setting is recognisable and everyday
- The woman looks like someone the viewer could know
- The lighting is natural, not processed
- The outfit is easy to imagine wearing yourself
- Nothing looks staged or fake

---

## Realism Rules

These are non-negotiable for every prompt. Realism is a performance variable — images that look like AI renders do not convert.

- Natural skin texture must remain visible — pores, fine lines, and subtle imperfections are correct
- Fabric must show realistic folds, weight, and natural movement — not a perfect render
- Lighting must be imperfect but flattering — natural daylight, not studio flash
- Background must be realistic, continuous, and have real depth — not blurred to white or clearly CGI
- No plastic skin
- No hyper-smooth AI surfaces
- No distorted hands — choose poses that reduce hand complexity where possible
- No fake logos, supplier branding, or watermarks
- No unreadable or floating text in the image
- No extra accessories, jewellery, or props unless they fit the setting naturally

---

## Performance Context

These prompts are for Meta CBO ad testing. The image must work as an ad, not as art.

- The product must be clearly visible and easy to see in the frame
- The buying reason must be readable from the image without needing the caption
- The woman must be someone the viewer can see themselves in — not someone they aspire to become
- The setting must be immediately recognisable and relatable — not exotic or staged
- The overall image must look like a genuine photo someone shared, not an ad someone made

---

## Brand Mood Reference Words

Use these as a tonal guide when writing prompts:

Calm. Warm. Real. Feminine. Easy. Confident. Wearable. Everyday. Polished. Comfortable. Approachable. Australian. Natural.
