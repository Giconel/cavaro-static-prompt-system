# Overlay and Safe Zone Rules

## Format

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical
- Dimensions: 1080 × 1920 pixels
- Platform: Meta (Instagram Feed, Instagram Stories, Facebook Feed, Reels cover)

---

## Instagram and Meta Safe Zones

Meta UI elements cover the top and bottom of the frame. Text placed in these zones will be hidden or look unprofessional.

**Exclusion zones — no text allowed:**

| Zone | Pixel range | What covers it |
|---|---|---|
| Top | 0–400px | Profile name, story UI, ad label |
| Bottom | 1520–1920px | CTA button, caption, navigation |
| Left edge | 0–150px | Clipped on feed |
| Right edge | 930–1080px | Clipped on feed |

**Safe text area:**
- Horizontal: 150px to 930px (780px wide)
- Vertical: 400px to 1520px (1120px tall)
- Centre of frame only

The subject of the image should be centred and fill the safe area comfortably. Product must be clearly visible in the middle third of the frame.

---

## Overlay Text Rules

**Purpose**
The overlay is a single hook-style text line that reinforces the ad angle. It is not the primary text or headline — it is a short visual hook on the image itself.

**Placement**
- Place text in the upper-centre or lower-centre of the safe zone
- Never place text directly over the garment or the woman's face
- Prefer placing text in a clear area of background (sky, wall, footpath)
- One line of text only — do not stack multiple lines of body copy on the image

**Content**
- If a hook is provided with the angle, use it — but still vary the hook across the 5 options where possible
- If no hook is provided, generate one for each option based on the angle and the specific moment in that image
- Do not use `[INSERT HOOK HERE]` as a placeholder — always write a real hook
- Keep it short — 4 to 6 words maximum
- Each of the 5 options should test a different hook angle — the same product and angle can be communicated many ways
- The hook must communicate one clear buying reason: comfort, fit, ease, confidence, or getting dressed simply
- No emojis, no hashtags, no URLs, no price callouts, no exclamation marks

**Hook writing approach:**
- Write from the customer's internal feeling, not from a brand announcement
- Examples of the right tone: "Dressed in under five minutes." / "Still wearing this at 4pm." / "The layer that goes with everything." / "Comfortable without trying."
- Examples of the wrong tone: "Shop our new arrivals!" / "Limited stock!" / "Feel amazing today!"

## Typography Rules

**Style**
- Elegant serif (e.g. Garamond, Cormorant, Playfair Display style) for a refined, feminine feel
- Or clean modern sans-serif (e.g. Helvetica Neue, Futura, Lato style) for a calm, minimal feel
- Match the typography weight to the mood of the angle — lighter and thinner for soft angles, slightly bolder for confident angles

**Colours**
Approved text colours:
- Warm white / cream
- Soft charcoal
- Muted warm beige
- Deep warm navy (if background is light)

Avoid:
- Bright white on white backgrounds
- Black harsh text
- Coloured text (red, pink, orange, green)
- Drop shadows that look cheap
- Glowing or outlined text effects

**Size**
- Large enough to read clearly on a mobile screen
- Do not make text so large it dominates the image
- Do not make text so small it is hard to read at feed size

---

## What To Avoid

- Canva-style graphics, sticker effects, or sale banners
- Multiple text elements or blocks of copy on the image
- Discount callouts ("50% off", "Sale now on")
- Emojis or decorative elements
- Text placed over the subject or garment
- Text in the exclusion zones
- Text that looks like it was added in a basic editor
