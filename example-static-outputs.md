# Example Static Outputs

Two worked examples in compact production format. Use these as reference for structure, prompt depth, and tone.

---

## Example 1

**Input — Product:** Relaxed-fit knit cardigan in warm taupe. Ribbed texture throughout. Open front, no buttons. Long straight sleeves with ribbed cuffs. Hip length. V-neck.

**Input — Angle:** Comfort Without Looking Sloppy

---

### 1. Short Product Read

- **Product:** Knit cardigan — warm taupe — ribbed knit, medium weight, relaxed drape — open front, long sleeves, hip length, V-neck
- **Protect:** Open front must stay open (not closed or buttoned), ribbed texture must be visible on body and cuffs, hip length must not shorten
- **Angle:** Comfort Without Looking Sloppy — the cardigan looks cosy but intentional, not like loungewear

---

### 2. Five Ready-to-Paste Static Prompts

## Example 2

**Input — Product:** Fitted linen blouse in soft dusty pink. Short sleeves with slight flutter. Button-front placket with five small pearl-effect buttons. Relaxed collar. Slightly tapered at waist. Hip length. Solid colour.

**Input — Angle:** Easy Outfit For Busy Days

---

### 1. Short Product Read

- **Product:** Linen blouse — soft dusty pink — natural linen with visible crinkle — relaxed collar, short flutter sleeves, five small pearl-effect buttons on front placket, hip length, slightly tapered at waist
- **Protect:** Pearl button detail (count, colour, size), flutter sleeve shape, natural linen crinkle texture — all three commonly misrendered
- **Angle:** Easy Outfit For Busy Days — she looks put-together without effort, the blouse carries the day for her

---

### 2. Five Ready-to-Paste Static Prompts
