# Target Customer Rules

## Who She Is

An Australian woman aged 35–55. She is a real person, not a model.

She buys clothes she can actually wear. She values comfort, quality, a flattering fit, and not having to think too hard about getting dressed. She is not chasing trends. She wants to look put-together without trying too hard.

---

## How She Should Look In The Image

**Age**
- She should appear to be in her late 30s to early 50s
- Do not make her look younger to seem more aspirational
- Do not make her look older than the target range

**Skin**
- Natural skin texture — pores, fine lines, and subtle imperfections are correct
- No plastic skin, no hyper-smoothed AI skin, no excessive retouching effect
- Warm, healthy complexion appropriate to the Australian lifestyle
- Ethnicity: default to a realistic Australian range — Anglo-Celtic, Southern European, East Asian, or mixed — unless the product or angle calls for something specific

**Body**
- Normal, realistic body proportions
- She does not need to be sample-size or model-thin
- She should not be plus-size as a visual focus either — this is about being believable, not a statement
- Relaxed, natural posture

**Hair**
- Natural-looking hair — not salon-perfect
- Could be mid-length, shoulder-length, or tied back simply
- Slight movement or natural texture is good
- Avoid overly styled, glamorous, or editorial hair

**Makeup**
- Minimal or no visible makeup
- Light tinted moisturiser effect at most
- No full glam, no contouring, no dramatic eye looks
- She looks naturally good, not made up

**Expression**
- Relaxed, soft, and natural
- A slight smile or neutral expression works
- Avoid: big posed smiles, smoldering looks, blank model stares, or theatrical expressions
- She can be mid-movement, mid-step, or slightly distracted — candid is good

**Pose and Movement**
- Natural and casual — not posed
- Walking, pausing, holding a coffee cup, looking at something slightly off camera
- Avoid: direct camera stare with a model pose, hands-on-hips fashion poses, exaggerated movement

---

## What She Is Not

- A professional model
- An influencer
- A Gen Z woman styled for social media
- An aspirational fantasy figure
- A stock photo woman
- A luxury fashion campaign model

---

## Believability Test

Before finalising the subject description, ask: would a real 40–50 year old Australian woman look at this image and think "that looks like someone I know"?

If yes — correct.
If no — adjust.
